Original concept art for a research-oriented turn-based narrative investigation micro-RPG titled The Sealed Lighthouse. Wide 3:2 composition. A storm-dark northern harbor at blue hour, a sealed stone lighthouse on a black reef, warm lantern paths connecting a weathered dock, evidence markers subtly embedded as brass geometric motifs, one small investigator silhouette for scale. Painterly gouache and etched storybook texture, restrained navy teal amber and oxidized copper palette, readable silhouettes, atmospheric but not horror, no combat, no logos, no typography, no UI, no copyrighted characters, production concept sheet quality.

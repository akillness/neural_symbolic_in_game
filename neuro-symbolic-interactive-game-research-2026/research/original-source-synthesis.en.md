## Corrections

- The twelve/eight-week schedules are historical plans as of 2026-08-12, not evidence of progress.
- `NPC Mind` and other leads without a primary source are excluded from citations.
- Tension, engagement, stress, excitement, and flow are not treated as interchangeable constructs.
- The 1.5–2.5 second lag and five-second smoothing window become pilot-estimated hyperparameters.
- Human `N<=10` is suitable for usability/pilot evidence, not broad effect claims.
- Graph, vector, operational, and trace storage responsibilities are separated by contracts.

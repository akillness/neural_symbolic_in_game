\begin{abstract}
Fluent game dialogue does not establish executable, authorized state transitions. \system{} treats generated events as untrusted proposals: strict parsing, external policy, seven predicates in six families, bounded repair, and pre-commit revalidation gate mutation. Typed-error repair $\rho$ reads only the candidate/errors, never authoritative state. Unkeyed SHA-256 links, semantic replay, and complete accounting audit outcomes. Separate evidence covers one-world offline conformance, single-model live screening, non-authoritative closed-world graph simulation, and participant-free Godot engineering. Offline evidence is mechanism-only. At $K{=}1$, guided/blind commits were 5/5 versus 0/5 only in a constructed repairable policy-blind regime. No lane establishes population efficacy, model superiority, player experience, or commercial-engine performance.
\end{abstract}

game AI, interactive narrative, symbolic validation, constrained generation, reproducibility
\end{IEEEkeywords}

\section{Introduction}
\IEEEPARstart{I}{nteractive} generation can require absent items, assert facts unknown to the speaker, disclose forbidden quest information, introduce unauthorized effects, or regress quest stages. Executable text environments distinguish plausible utterances from valid transitions \cite{S11_cote2019textworld,S12_wang2022scienceworld}; grounded dialogue and personalized quests demonstrate useful quest, entity, and graph context \cite{S03_weir2024ontologically,S20_ashby2023personalized}, not mutation authority. \textbf{\system{} treats generated events as untrusted transaction proposals; only an external deterministic commit gate permits canonical mutation} (Fig.~\ref{fig:architecture}).

\section{Problem Definition and Guarantee Boundary}
Validation input at step $t$ is $c_t=(G_t,q_t)$: typed snapshot fields $G_t$ and externally authored action-policy, disclosure, and quest-stage constraints $q_t$ stored with them. Terminal history $h_{<t}$ is separate from \texttt{WorldState}; predicates never read it, nor is state reconstructed from logs. An accepted one-step transition updates $G_t$ under unchanged $q_t$; policy-version rotation is out of scope. Retrieval, narrative scores, model rationales, memory summaries, and future affect estimates are non-authoritative proposal context.

An untrusted proposer emits candidate $a_t$. Strict parsing first enforces the shared candidate-key contract, including rejection of unknown top-level fields, and establishes the declared schema. The validator returns
\begin{equation}
V(c_t,a_t)=(v_{\mathrm{policy}},v_{\mathrm{pre}},v_{\mathrm{reach}},
v_{\mathrm{know}},v_{\mathrm{disc}},v_{\mathrm{elig}},v_{\mathrm{stage}},E_t),
\end{equation}
where $E_t$ is a structured error set. Mutation is
\begin{equation}
c_{t+1}=\begin{cases}
T(c_t,a_t), & \bigwedge_{i=1}^{7} v_i=1,\\
c_t, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\label{eq:transition}
\end{equation}

Deterministic valid/invalid/repair/fallback/replay/fault fixtures test four implementation invariants, not general theorems. \textbf{I1:} exhausted validation or pre-commit proposal/controller failure returns the prior state. \textbf{I2:} returning callbacks and $K\geq0$ imply at most $K+1$ recorded attempts plus a successful commit's defensive validation; without enforced callback deadlines, this is not wall-clock termination. \textbf{I3:} accepted \texttt{CandidateAction.effects} lie within \texttt{ActionPolicy.allowed\_effects}, and policy explicitly authorizes quest-stage targets. \textbf{I4:} frozen schemas/deterministic transitions let accepted traces reconstruct their stated terminal state.

These invariants assume correct policy authorship, faithful extraction into declared candidate fields, deterministic software versions, returning callbacks, and single-process file ownership. Replay revalidates the recorded structured candidate and transition; it does \emph{not} verify the provenance, prompts, or stochastic generation of intermediate repairs, and natural-language implications omitted from structured fields remain outside the guarantee.

Repair cannot mutate state; exhaustion returns unchanged fallback. The typed-error operator (\texttt{counterexample\_guided\_repair}) reads only candidate $a$ and errors $E$ (its sole state-derived input), never authoritative \texttt{WorldState}:
\begin{equation}
\rho(a,E)=a \oplus \textstyle\bigcup_{e\in E}\delta(e),
\label{eq:rho}
\end{equation}
Table~\ref{tab:rho-rules} defines deterministic edits $\delta(e)$; $\oplus$ applies them. At most one field changes per error entity, hence at most $|E|$ fields per attempt. The fixed-point property holds only for identical $E$, not changing errors or convergence. Conflicting requirements/rejections on one field are irreparable and untouched. The state-reading reference ignores errors and reconstructs preconditions/effects/both quest-stage fields from authoritative state. It never edits required objects/used facts/disclosed facts: deleting these declarations would launder violations, not repair them. This oracle is not deployable-repair evidence.

\subsection{Integrity, Semantic Replay, and Accounting}\label{sec:audit}
Unkeyed SHA-256 row checksums and proposal--trace links detect accidental/tested changes, not signatures, writer identity, or joint content/checksum rewriting. Replay strictly parses prior state/candidate/validation pairs, requires invalid nonfinal attempts, recomputes the terminal transition, compares final state, and checks adjacent JSONL continuity. It never reruns repair generation. Request$\rightarrow$decision$\rightarrow$result order and first-issued-action/exactly-one-receipt binding imply neither duplicate-action-ID issuance prohibition nor cryptographic authentication.

Terminal classes are commit, symbolic fallback, adapter failure, and controller failure. The latter preserves state/assignment even without a completed candidate trace; complete accounting need not mean a trace per row. Pre-execution keys bind run/arm/scenario/seed/model/revision and controller-configuration/input/prior-state hashes. Aggregation rejects duplicate/missing/unexpected keys and separates symbolic/adapter/controller failures.

\section{Discussion}
\subsection{Established Scope}
Mechanism fixtures test state isolation, repair/fallback, corruption rejection, and row classification, not detector attribution. NPC counts (Sec.~\ref{sec:lanes}) imply no efficacy, superiority, generalization, human benefit, cost equality, semantic gold, or accuracy ceiling.

State-reading handles oracle-only cases; $\rho$ matches guided-repairable cases only. Live null regimes, unpinned revision, and quasi-replication preclude sample-efficiency/model-capability claims. Unlike semantic repair, $\rho$ consumes deterministic pre-execution errors with failed arms isolated \cite{S45_madaan2023selfrefine,S48_shinn2023reflexion,S46_chen2023selfdebug,S47_solarlezama2006sketching}.
\noindent\textbf{Disclosure interpretation.} \texttt{general\_rule}/\texttt{ontology\_gate} parity is frozen-case consistency, not ontology superiority/completeness/necessity, latent-state inference, or arbitrary-prose leakage freedom. Rule-change fixtures diagnose edit scope/missed paired cases.

\subsection{Relationship to Prior Work}

Planned, not executed: preregistered multi-model/oracle/blind-retry comparisons, matched-validity retrieval/memory ablations, and blinded matched-validity human study with justified power/mixed-effects; screening substitutes for none \cite{S27_vanderlee2019best,S31_agarwal2021precipice,S35_barr2013random}.

\section{Threats, Ethics, and Artifact Scope}
Nine boundaries apply: \textbf{L1:} encoded fields miss semantics and narrative quality is unmeasured \cite{S55_huang2026beyond}; \textbf{L2:} one world/live state gives no cross-world, cross-policy, or scale generalization; \textbf{L3:} state-reading is an oracle and classes/regimes do not establish repair quality or sample efficiency; \textbf{L4:} one hosted proposer has unpinned revision/seeds and no token accounting, while E2 has no Python--Godot authorization round trip; \textbf{L5:} no participant/affect or comprehension/agency/enjoyment/immersion/benefit evidence; \textbf{L6:} unkeyed hashes provide integrity, not authentication or tamper resistance; \textbf{L7:} concurrency is unverified under single-writer consistency; \textbf{L8:} one startup-dominated Godot fixture has samples above 16.7\,ms, so portability/performance are untested; \textbf{L9:} selected fixtures/cells support no population, safety-rate, or model-ranking inference. Internal validity requires independent policy expectations/no leakage; reproducibility requires pinned software/artifacts.
NPC-DISC-1 covers only frozen IDs/templates/state axes, not free-form/implicit, hidden-payload, or all-UI-channel leakage. Declaration-derived expectations are neither human gold nor holdout; Jev has no label/commit authority.
NPC deadline/duplicate/stale/late-drop/budget/binding checks are protocol, not latency/quality evidence. Cancellation is local-only; \texttt{core.cancel} is unit/controller-only, not human/cross-world/independent-gold/C6 evidence. No participants/personal telemetry were collected. Future human/affect work requires ethics review, consent, compensation disclosure, minimization, retention/deletion, opt-out, and calibrated/bias-controlled auxiliary judges \cite{S27_vanderlee2019best,S28_karpinska2021perils,S29_zheng2023judging,S30_chen2024judgement}.

\section{Conclusion}
\system{} gates mutation and audits linked replay. Fixtures show conformance; pilot screening separates $\rho$/blind retry (5/5 vs. 0/5) only in the constructed repairable regime. NPC-DISC-1 adds closed \texttt{reveal\_hint}/three-arm/finite-strata/redisplay-no-mutation evidence, not gold or efficacy. Cross-model/player/retrieval/memory/affect/commercial benefits remain untested; Sec.~\ref{sec:lanes} separates additive NPC, historical \texttt{native012}, and legacy ENG1.

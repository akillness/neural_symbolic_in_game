\begin{abstract}
\system{} combines external authorization, typed-error repair, unchanged fallback, revalidation, replay, and accounting; its read-only bilingual ontology view binds source declarations to public observations without mutation authority. One operator-assisted Codex dashboard case ($n{=}1$, independent repeats $0$) reports \texttt{gpt-6.1-sol}, repaired geometry, retained failures, 62 checks, a Godot Web build, and real-input denial/disclosure/redisplay; provider identity, revision, seed, cost, request count, and inference latency remain unavailable. A fixed-game native pilot on one authored quest records 8 assigned rows (7 observed): 5 system-goal successes, one retained pre-response harness-serializer technical failure (executed \texttt{null}, not rerun), and 2 \texttt{gpt-6.1-sol} ontology response-timeout failures; 23 host invocations, 0 repairs, 0/16 unsafe committed transitions, 7 valid semantic-link replays, and 6 valid fresh-engine reexecutions (1 \texttt{NOT\_RUN}; first row unobserved). It is exploratory, with one stateless intent proposer and deterministic planner/checker; it does not establish A/C, ontology efficacy, ranking, noninferiority, or player benefit.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem Definition and Guarantee Boundary}
$c_t=(G_t,q_t)$ stores typed snapshot $G_t$ and external action-policy/disclosure/quest-stage constraints. Predicates neither read $h_{<t}$ nor reconstruct \texttt{WorldState}; transitions update $G_t$ only, excluding policy rotation. Retrieval, scores, rationales, memory, and future affect lack authority.

Strict parsing of untrusted candidate $a_t$ enforces the shared key/schema contract, rejecting unknown top-level fields. Validation returns
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
V(c_t,a_t)&=(\boldsymbol{v},E_t),\\
\boldsymbol{v}&=(v_{\mathrm{policy}},v_{\mathrm{pre}},v_{\mathrm{reach}},v_{\mathrm{know}},\\
&\qquad v_{\mathrm{disc}},v_{\mathrm{elig}},v_{\mathrm{stage}})\in\{0,1\}^{7},
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
where $E_t$ is a structured error set. Mutation is
\begin{equation}
c_{t+1}=\begin{cases}
T(c_t,a_t), & \bigwedge_{i=1}^{7}(v_i=1),\\
c_t, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\label{eq:transition}
\end{equation}

Fixtures test invariants, not theorems. \textbf{I1:} validation exhaustion or pre-commit proposal/controller failure preserves state. \textbf{I2:} returning callbacks and $K\geq0$ bound attempts to $K+1$ with pre-commit revalidation; without deadlines, wall-clock termination is not guaranteed. \textbf{I3:} accepted \texttt{CandidateAction.effects} stay within \texttt{ActionPolicy.allowed\_effects}, including authorized quest-stage targets. \textbf{I4:} frozen schemas/deterministic transitions enable terminal-state reconstruction.

Assumptions: correct policy/extraction, deterministic versions, returning callbacks, single-process ownership. Replay checks structured transitions, not repair provenance, prompts, stochastic generation, or undeclared natural-language implications.

Repair cannot mutate state; exhaustion preserves it. Typed \texttt{counterexample\_guided\_repair} reads only candidate $a$/errors $E$ (its sole state-derived input), never \texttt{WorldState}:
\begin{equation}
\rho(a,E)=a\oplus\bigcup_{e\in E}\delta(e),
\label{eq:rho}
\end{equation}
Table~\ref{tab:rho-rules}'s $\oplus$ applies deterministic $\delta(e)$: one field/error entity, at most $|E|$/attempt; conflicting requirements/rejections leave fields unchanged. Fixed points require identical $E$, not convergence. The nondeployable state-reading oracle ignores errors, reconstructs preconditions/effects and both quest-stage fields from state, and never launders violations by deleting required objects/used facts/disclosed facts.

Unkeyed SHA-256 detects accidental/tested changes, not identity or joint rewriting. Request$\rightarrow$decision$\rightarrow$result and first-action/one-receipt bindings do not prevent duplicate IDs or impersonation. \section{Discussion, Threats, Ethics, and Artifact Scope}
Semantic repair \cite{S45_madaan2023selfrefine,S48_shinn2023reflexion,S46_chen2023selfdebug,S47_solarlezama2006sketching} uses pre-execution errors; the oracle only reads state. Rule agreement/edit-scope diagnostics are neither capability nor ontology superiority.
Implementation confound: row 1 failed under controller-v3 before a model response; rows 2--8 used controller-v4 after a one-line JSON-boundary correction with unchanged model-visible inputs, so selector/representation differences are not model-attributable.


The fixed-game pilot is bounded native evidence; no ontology-efficacy test ran. A (model/stack consistency) and C (fresh-reference noninferiority) remain unexecuted and require budget-bounded completion under fixed source/policy, sealed assignments, exact identity/revision fields, and separate safety/replay accounting; it answers neither. Endpoint time is request/evidence receipt to the first receipt passing frozen mandatory checks (time-to-first-all-predeclared-validation-pass); unfinished/timeout/failure/stage-not-reached runs have no successful time.

\textbf{Threats and boundaries.} The native pilot has one authored quest and two repeats per cell; comparator power, neutral complete-attempt inventories, and independent traceability scores are absent. State consistency implies neither behavioral nor narrative coherence \cite{S55_huang2026beyond}. Predicate/role/Godot/DISC sets are not latent states; declarations are not human gold/holdouts; lanes retain separate denominators. No participant, affect, comprehension, agency, enjoyment, immersion, benefit, population, safety-rate, model-ranking, or commercial-performance inference follows. Hashes do not authenticate; replay equality is not semantic correctness. Human/affect studies require ethics review, consent, compensation disclosure, minimization, retention/deletion, opt-out, and calibrated judges \cite{S27_vanderlee2019best,S28_karpinska2021perils,S29_zheng2023judging,S30_chen2024judgement}.

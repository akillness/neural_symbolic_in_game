\begin{abstract}
\system{} combines external authorization, typed-error repair, unchanged fallback, revalidation, replay, and accounting for generated game events. Its read-only bilingual ontology view binds source declarations to public observations without mutation authority. One operator-assisted Codex dashboard case ($n{=}1$, independent repeats $0$) reports runtime \texttt{gpt-6.1-sol}, repaired geometry, retained failures, 62 checks, a Godot Web build, and real-input denial/disclosure/redisplay. Provider identity, revision, seed, cost, and inference latency remain unavailable. This bounded integration evidence is not model comparison, ontology superiority, or player benefit; traceability and whole-game/repeated-cross-model studies remain proposed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem Definition and Guarantee Boundary}
$c_t=(G_t,q_t)$ stores typed snapshot $G_t$ and external action-policy/disclosure/quest-stage constraints $q_t$. Predicates neither read $h_{<t}$ nor reconstruct \texttt{WorldState} from logs; transitions update $G_t$, not $q_t$, and exclude policy rotation. Retrieval, scores, rationales, memory, and future affect have no authority.

Strict parsing of untrusted candidate $a_t$ enforces the shared key/schema contract, rejecting unknown top-level fields. Validation returns
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
V(c_t,a_t)&=(\boldsymbol{v},E_t),\\
\boldsymbol{v}&=(v_{\mathrm{policy}},v_{\mathrm{pre}},v_{\mathrm{reach}},v_{\mathrm{know}},\\
&\qquad v_{\mathrm{disc}},v_{\mathrm{elig}},v_{\mathrm{stage}})\in\{0,1\}^{7},
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
where $E_t$ is a structured error set. Mutation is
\begin{equation}
c_{t+1}=\begin{cases}
T(c_t,a_t), & \bigwedge_{i=1}^{7}(v_i=1),\\
c_t, & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\label{eq:transition}
\end{equation}

Fixtures test implementation invariants, not theorems. \textbf{I1:} validation exhaustion or pre-commit proposal/controller failure preserves state. \textbf{I2:} returning callbacks and $K\geq0$ bound attempts to $K+1$ with pre-commit revalidation; without enforced deadlines, wall-clock termination is not guaranteed. \textbf{I3:} accepted \texttt{CandidateAction.effects} stay within \texttt{ActionPolicy.allowed\_effects}, including authorized quest-stage targets. \textbf{I4:} frozen schemas/deterministic transitions enable terminal-state reconstruction.

Assumptions: correct policy, faithful extraction, deterministic versions, returning callbacks, and single-process file ownership. Replay checks structured transitions, not repair provenance, prompts, stochastic generation, or undeclared natural-language implications.

Repair cannot mutate state; exhaustion preserves it. Typed \texttt{counterexample\_guided\_repair} reads only candidate $a$/errors $E$ (its sole state-derived input), never \texttt{WorldState}:
\begin{equation}
\rho(a,E)=a\oplus\bigcup_{e\in E}\delta(e),
\label{eq:rho}
\end{equation}
Table~\ref{tab:rho-rules}'s $\oplus$ applies deterministic $\delta(e)$: one field/error entity, at most $|E|$/attempt; conflicting requirements/rejections leave fields unchanged. Fixed points require identical $E$, not convergence. The nondeployable state-reading oracle ignores errors, reconstructs preconditions/effects/both quest-stage fields from state, and never launders violations by deleting required objects/used facts/disclosed facts.

\subsection{Integrity, Semantic Replay, and Accounting}\label{sec:audit}
Unkeyed SHA-256 checksums/trace links detect accidental/tested changes, not writer identity or joint rewriting. Replay parses prior state/candidate/validation, requires invalid nonfinal attempts, recomputes final state, checks equality/JSONL continuity, and never reruns repair. Request$\rightarrow$decision$\rightarrow$result and first-issued-action/exactly-one-receipt binding do not prevent duplicate IDs or impersonation. Terminal classes are commit, symbolic fallback, adapter failure, and controller failure; the latter preserves state/assignment without a completed proposal trace. Pre-execution keys bind run, arm, scenario, seed, model, revision, configuration/input/prior-state hashes; aggregation rejects duplicate/missing/unexpected keys and separates failures.

\section{Discussion, Threats, Ethics, and Artifact Scope}
Semantic repair \cite{S45_madaan2023selfrefine,S48_shinn2023reflexion,S46_chen2023selfdebug,S47_solarlezama2006sketching} addresses iterative correction; $\rho$ uses deterministic pre-execution errors and oracle-only handles state reading. Frozen-case rule agreement and edit-scope diagnostics are neither capability nor ontology superiority.


Unexecuted plans cover matched-information whole-game comparisons, repeated cross-model runs with exact revisions, independent semantic oracles, and a justified blinded human study (locators below). \subsection{Proposed traceability instrument (not measured)}
The proposed construct is recoverable requirement/artifact/action/outcome correspondence, not correctness, hidden reasoning, developer skill, or player benefit. Freeze neutral $U_d$/links $L_d(u)$; independent assessors award 1 only after exact source/version, every raw-evidence relation, and a locator are verified. Report $n_d/N_d$, disagreement, and seeded-corruption controls, not weighted composites; incomplete inventories are indeterminate. Missing/inaccessible/stale/contradicted/unverified links earn 0 with reasons; verified zero repairs may be N/A, but empty logs are not zero. Report PASS, FAIL, NOT\_RUN, STAGE\_NOT\_REACHED, OUT\_OF\_SCOPE, and MISSING separately; \texttt{stage\_not\_reached} is never PASS.

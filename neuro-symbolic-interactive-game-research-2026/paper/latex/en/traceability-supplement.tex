\section{Proposed traceability instrument (not measured)}
Traceability tests recover requirement/artifact/action/outcome correspondence, not correctness, hidden reasoning, developer skill, or player benefit. Freeze neutral $U_d$/links $L_d(u)$; independent assessors award 1 only for exact source/version, all raw-evidence relations, and locator; otherwise 0 with missing/inaccessible/stale/contradicted/unverified reasons. Report $n_d/N_d$, disagreement, and seeded-corruption controls; no weighted composites; incomplete inventories are indeterminate. Verified zero repairs may be N/A; empty logs are not zero. Distinguish PASS, FAIL, NOT\_RUN, STAGE\_NOT\_REACHED, OUT\_OF\_SCOPE, and MISSING; \texttt{stage\_not\_reached} never passes.

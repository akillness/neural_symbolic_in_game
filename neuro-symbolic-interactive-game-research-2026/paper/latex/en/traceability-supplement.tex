\section{Proposed traceability instrument (not measured)}
The proposed instrument assesses requirement/artifact/action/outcome correspondence, not correctness, hidden reasoning, developer skill, or player benefit. For each dimension $d$, freeze an arm-neutral inventory $U_d$ and the required links $L_d(u)$ for each unit $u$. Let $N_d$ count applicable units in $U_d$, and $n_d$ count units with every required link verified. Independent assessors award 1 only when exact source/version, raw-evidence relations, and locator are verified; otherwise they award 0 and record missing, inaccessible, stale, contradicted, or unverified evidence. Report $n_d/N_d$ only with a complete inventory and $N_d>0$, alongside disagreement and seeded-corruption controls; do not form weighted composites. Mark incomplete inventories INDETERMINATE and report observed counts and the gap. N/A requires independent evidence of non-applicability. Independently verified zero actual repair attempts can qualify a repair item, but an empty log cannot. Keep PASS, FAIL, NOT\_RUN, STAGE\_NOT\_REACHED, OUT\_OF\_SCOPE, and MISSING distinct; \texttt{stage\_not\_reached} never passes.

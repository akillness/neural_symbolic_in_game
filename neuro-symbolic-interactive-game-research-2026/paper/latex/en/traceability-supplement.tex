\section{Proposed traceability instrument (not measured)}
The proposed instrument assesses correspondence across requirements, artifacts, actions, and outcomes rather than correctness, hidden reasoning, developer skill, or player benefit. For each dimension $d$, the protocol freezes an arm-neutral inventory $U_d$ and the required links $L_d(u)$ for each unit $u$, letting $N_d$ count applicable units in $U_d$ and $n_d$ count units with every required link verified. Independent assessors award 1 only when the exact source and version, raw-evidence relations, and locator are all verified; otherwise they award 0 and classify the gap as missing, inaccessible, stale, contradicted, or unverified evidence. The ratio $n_d/N_d$ is reported only when the inventory is complete and $N_d>0$, alongside assessor disagreement and seeded-corruption controls, without forming weighted composites. Incomplete inventories are marked INDETERMINATE together with observed counts and the inventory gap, and an N/A designation requires independent evidence of non-applicability. Independently verified zero actual repair attempts can qualify a repair item, whereas an empty log cannot. Finally, PASS, FAIL, NOT\_RUN, STAGE\_NOT\_REACHED, OUT\_OF\_SCOPE, and MISSING must remain distinct, and \texttt{stage\_not\_reached} never counts as a pass.

\section{Threats to Validity, Limitations, and Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
\textbf{Limitations and threats to validity.} Across five quest worlds, live cross-family closed-loop Godot~4.7.1 execution, 480 Datalog depth-scaling runs, and 540 neural Stage-2 escape evaluations, six explicit boundaries remain. First, all live CLI evaluations (\texttt{agy} \texttt{cli\_version~1.5.11--1.5.12} for Gemini and GPT-OSS, plus \texttt{claude-code-cli} for \textbf{P8} and Cohort~B Claude runs after \texttt{agy} hit HTTP~429 after 55 \textbf{P8} items) record unattested provider revisions, unseeded decoding, and every parse failure (2 GPT-OSS turns in \textbf{P6}, 3 GPT-OSS tasks in \textbf{P7}, 2 GPT-OSS episodes in \textbf{P10} Cohort~A, 1 Flash case in \textbf{P8}) in full denominators; \texttt{gpt-\allowbreak oss-\allowbreak 120b-\allowbreak medium} is left explicitly unevaluated on \textbf{P8} and \textbf{P10} Cohort~B due to HTTP~429 exhaustion. Second, three Phase-3 protocol confounds block unconditional generalization claims: (i) in \textbf{P7} (and Phase-2 \textbf{P4}), the stateless $K{=}1$ LLM regeneration prompt omitted failed candidate $a$, so 1/30 reflects candidate-blind regeneration rather than an apples-to-apples repair baseline; (ii) in \textbf{P6}, \texttt{gpt-oss} deadlocked at Turn~1 (\texttt{UNKNOWN\_ACTION\_TYPE} with 0 commits / 0 flat leaks) under a fixed 3-turn script; and (iii) \textbf{P10} Cohort~B guided prompts supplied target actions, \texttt{actor\_npc\_knowledge}, and initial shorthand omissions to test deterministic Godot~4.7.1 + $\rho$ execution rather than unguided autonomous play. Third, \textbf{P8} spans 2 model families (Anthropic + 2 Gemini variants) while \texttt{multihop\_ontology\_gate} resides in evaluation scripts rather than \texttt{src/nesy\_game/validator.py}. Fourth, the pre-registered 180-unit attested API protocol remains \texttt{0/180} (\texttt{NOT\_RUN/BLOCKED}). Fifth, all five quest worlds (up to 8 rooms, 3 roles) and $R{\le}64, d{\le}5$ procedural graphs remain compact finite-state machines rather than commercial 3D open-world games. Sixth, human evaluations remain unexecuted ($N{=}0$ players or raters): the blinded 36-item \texttt{TR01--TR06} packet (\texttt{AWAITING\_}\allowbreak\texttt{HUMAN\_}\allowbreak\texttt{RATERS}) carries zero fabricated labels, so usability, immersion, and human Krippendorff~$\alpha$ remain unmeasured and are explicitly excluded from our claims.

\textbf{Conclusion.} \system{} separates generative proposal authority from deterministic commit authority via a seven-predicate validator, state-blind counterexample repair $\rho(a,E)$, hash-linked semantic replay, and a read-only ontology observer (Theorems~1--4 for \textbf{I1--I4}). Phase-3 experiments demonstrate zero-leak safety across Claude~Sonnet~5.5, GPT-OSS~120B, Gemini~4~Hydrogen, and Gemini~3.8~Flash under explicit boundary conditions: 30/30 zero-leak scripted 3-agent negotiation with 53.3\% vs.\ 23.3\% milestones ($p{=}0.00391$; GPT-OSS Turn-1 deadlock preserved), 87/90 (96.7\%) cross-family parse yield with 0/87 unsafe commits and 30/30 zero-token $\rho$ repairs ($Q{=}191.28$), Fleiss' $\kappa{=}0.9614$ across 540 neural escape evaluations, 480/480 $O(d)$ Datalog-certified scaling up to $R{=}64, d{=}5$ ($\le$7.12\,$\mu$s median), and 50/50 deterministic 0-LLM Godot~4.7.1 replay parity (20/20 guided Cohort~B goals, 0/50 unsafe commits), packaged with \texttt{DATASHEET\_v3.md} and archive-local Croissant~1.0 v3 (17/17 SHA-256 digests).

# Human Study and Data Protocol — Design Only

Status: `[TARGET]`; recruitment and collection prohibited in Cycle 1

Before a later study, record jurisdiction-specific review status, consent version, eligibility,
compensation, randomization, blinding, attention checks, withdrawal, retention, deletion, breach
response, and contact route. Use participant pseudonyms that cannot be reversed inside the research
repository. Store consent/identity data outside experiment traces under separately approved access.

Cycle 1 may build schemas and a blank UI only. It may not enroll, record a real participant ID,
collect free-form personal text, or turn synthetic-player output into human evidence.

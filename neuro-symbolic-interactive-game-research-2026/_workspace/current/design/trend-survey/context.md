## Product context / 제품 맥락

*The Sealed Lighthouse* is an `8–12 min` turn-based narrative investigation micro-RPG built as a
paper-citable systems probe. [TARGET] It is not intended to compete with the content volume of the
surveyed commercial games. The relevant comparison surface is the compact interaction grammar:
observe, select evidence, question an NPC, attempt a gated action, receive legible feedback, and
continue from a canonical state.

수사 마이크로 RPG다. [TARGET] 비교 상용작의 콘텐츠 규모와 경쟁하지 않으며, 관찰·증거 선택·NPC

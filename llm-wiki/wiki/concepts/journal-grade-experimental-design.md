# Journal-Grade Experimental Design

The planned SCI/SCIE submission uses a two-stage design. Ten models are screened exploratorily; a frozen Pareto rule promotes three representative models. Confirmatory experiments cross those models with six controller systems over world generation, NPC dialogue, and affect adaptation, while holding out worlds, quest motifs, NPC identities, and relation motifs.

One primary endpoint is registered per track. Pilot variance informs prospective power simulation. Confirmatory analysis reports effect sizes, 95% intervals, multiplicity adjustment, mixed model structure, diagnostics, exclusions, and sensitivity analysis. Human evaluation is randomized and blinded, compares outputs at matched validity, and cannot be replaced with synthetic agents.

Formal and semantic validation are distinct. Reproducibility requires exact revisions, prompt/config hashes, hardware/energy data, deterministic replay, negative results, and a trace-to-table manifest.

The first target candidate is IEEE Transactions on Games; venue choice is re-evaluated after results and SCIE coverage is checked in Clarivate's Master Journal List at submission time.

# TRACE-RPG Experimental Game Track — The Sealed Lighthouse

Status: **APPROVED DESIGN + HEADLESS IMPLEMENTATION TRACK; NO EFFICACY RESULT**

On 2026-08-13 the user completed a standard deep interview and fixed five decisions: a turn-based
narrative investigation micro-RPG; planning plus a minimal headless slice; Godot 4.x; a structured
state/text primary track plus a frozen-image VLM/UI secondary track; and human-study tooling without
recruitment or data collection.

The resulting *Sealed Lighthouse* episode uses the existing harbor/Captain Mira seed. Its canonical
development path acquires a reachable signal lens, rejects an early forbidden and stage-gated
disclosure without mutation, installs the lens, permits the later tide-marks hint, saves and loads,
replays JSONL events, and injects duplicate/timeout faults. A separate corrupt-save negative fixture
verifies pre-mutation checksum rejection. This is an engine-local authored policy mirror, not a
live Python authorization round-trip or sampled game/model/player observation.

The game-studio workspace is single-live-folder `_workspace/current/`, governed by project
`CLAUDE.md` plus the root research contract. The studio roles are director, designer, PM,
programmer, and QA, with game-integrator and logic-auditor retaining independent high-risk review.
Every G1--G8 verdict needs measured value, method, and evidence path; absent evidence remains FIX.
A zero-economy research override replaces monetization assumptions.

The experiment protocol now exposes six controller arms: direct commit, structural constraint only,
validator rejection only, matched-budget blind retry, structured repair, and full TRACE-RPG. The
holdout groups world template, quest motif, NPC identity, relation motif, and disclosure pattern.
Production lore QA is explicitly separated from the independently authored hidden encoded and
semantic oracle.

Four concept surfaces were created with `god-tibo-imagen`: environment, Captain Mira, investigation
UI, and evidence icons. Independent visual audit rejected the first icon sheet for key/seal motifs
that contradicted its canonical-visible prompt; v1 remains in `rejected/` and v2 is active. Exact
prompts and prompt hashes, reference inputs, response IDs, observed dimensions, file sizes, SHA-256
values, curation state, and limitations are recorded. Image generation is prohibited during
experiment runtime. Frozen images are excluded from the primary track and may enter only the
`secondary-vlm-ui` track.

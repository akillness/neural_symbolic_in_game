# TRACE-RPG Academic Pipeline — Stages 2.5 and 3

The approved 36-source pool passed the pre-write integrity gate only after the present paper was
narrowed to a deterministic offline conformance study. The working title removes “affect-adaptive.”
Current contributions are the canonical transaction contract, encoded state-relative commit gate,
bounded counterexample repair, final-outcome semantic replay, and assignment-complete offline
experiment accounting.

All five efficacy claims `C-RESULT-001` through `C-RESULT-005` remain unsupported future hypotheses.
The current evidence is limited to 41 authored tests and a two-assignment frozen smoke fixture (one
commit, one recorded timeout). These observations do not estimate model quality, controller efficacy,
repair efficiency, player preference, affect performance, live latency, or engine portability.

Important design corrections:

- H1 needs an independent semantic outcome oracle; the intervention validator cannot be its sole judge.
- H2 needs an explicit blind-retry arm and per-attempt token/latency accounting.
- H3 needs implemented retrieval/memory and an independently audited contradiction metric.
- H4 needs an affect controller, independent labels, and a justified non-inferiority margin.
- H5 currently confounds one promoted model with each profile; any directions are instance-level only.
- SHA-256 is an unkeyed content checksum, not authentication.
- Current semantic replay reconstructs the final candidate/outcome and continuity, not every intermediate
  repair-generation operation.

Stage 3 proposes a ten-page IEEE Transactions on Games structure: introduction; related work; formal
problem/guarantee boundary; architecture/algorithms; designed offline pilot; generated pilot results;
discussion; explicitly future confirmatory extensions; threats/ethics/artifacts; conclusion. The main
paper uses three SVG figures and four generated tables. Unique designed fixtures, not repeated seeds,
are the pilot denominator; no population p-values are reported.

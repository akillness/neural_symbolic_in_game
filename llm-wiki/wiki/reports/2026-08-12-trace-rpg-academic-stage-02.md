# TRACE-RPG Academic Pipeline — Stage 2

As of 2026-08-12, the primary target is IEEE Transactions on Games. The working package is an
English IEEE Transactions manuscript plus a Korean companion, with both rendered to PDF. The
submission copy must be double-anonymous. The full paper targets ten pages and a 150--200-word
self-contained abstract, subject to a final live-guideline check before submission.

The Stage 2 shortlist contains 36 sources spanning 2011--2026. It separates peer-reviewed final
records from three preprints and one online/future-issue record. The evidence architecture covers
neuro-symbolic world validation, constrained decoding, graph and temporal memory, interactive game
benchmarks, NPC/player evaluation, affect modelling, statistical inference, and reproducibility.

Empirical boundary: the current repository can support only deterministic offline implementation
verification. Forty-one authored tests and a two-assignment frozen smoke fixture can show conformance
of the tested validator, repair, trace, schema, and accounting paths. They cannot establish H1--H5,
ten-model performance, human preference, affect accuracy, live-provider latency, or engine-level
performance. Such claims remain TODO until their declared studies run.

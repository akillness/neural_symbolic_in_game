# 2026-08-12 Research Synthesis

The original five documents were fully reviewed and restructured into one shared controller paper rather than two partially overlapping system papers plus an under-specified affect plan.

The synthesized research program is deliberately experimental: learned models propose world actions, dialogue, repairs, visual observations, or affect signals, while a deterministic event-sourced gate remains authoritative. Ten model profiles span compact/open-training/open-weight/world-model/VLM/hosted controls. Screening is exploratory; only a three-model preregistered confirmation can support paper conclusions.

The strongest contribution path for a SCI/SCIE game journal is cross-model evidence that a shared commit gate reduces hard failures without destroying valid-output diversity or real-time feasibility. Affect adaptation remains a conditional contribution and must demonstrate construct validity, human ground truth, uncertainty calibration, and no hard-validity regression.

Unresolved risks are semantic incompleteness of validators, model/API drift, proprietary lore/data restrictions, human-study power and ethics, and engine-level external validity. The harness encodes independent fact, logic, design, statistical, replay, and bilingual review loops.

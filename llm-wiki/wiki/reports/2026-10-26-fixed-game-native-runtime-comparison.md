## Reusable lesson / 재사용할 교훈

Validate the **exact CLI boundary** that will serialize configuration, admit frozen request bytes and dispatch to the native host; a passing injected callback or preparation path is not proof that this boundary works. Preserve assigned failures and original IDs through repairs. Record **whether replay ran separately from whether it was valid**: in the earlier native identity-extension cohort, row 4 is `NOT_RUN` and row 1 is `NOT_OBSERVED`; neither is `INVALID` or a pass. These are not the local Ollama row numbers reported above. **Frozen-byte admission must be an outcome-neutral integrity check, never a favorable-outcome filter**: timeout, refusal, technical failure and missingness stay in their assigned denominators with their actual nulls. Exact-byte verification is not scientific efficacy or provider-identity proof.

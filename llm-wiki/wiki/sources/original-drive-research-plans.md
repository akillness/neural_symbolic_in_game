# Original Drive Research Plans

Status: source summary  
Raw sources: `raw/sources/local/original-plans/` (five immutable Markdown files plus `SHA256SUMS`).

The archive contains five Markdown documents. The 12-week umbrella plan splits world generation (Paper A) and knowledge-grounded NPC dialogue (Paper B), while a later version compresses them to eight weeks. A separate FunQA-derived plan proposes video/tension/RL/RAG components. All numerical results in Paper A and B are placeholders rather than completed experiments.

Durable synthesis:

- World-state validation and NPC information policy share one state/event substrate and should be evaluated as one controller before splitting into downstream papers.
- Proposals, rejections, repairs, evidence IDs, and state transitions must be retained as first-class data.
- Tension-related delay, normalization, smoothing, and small-user-study choices in the source plan are hypotheses, not established constants.
- The proposed `NPC Mind` citation remains an unverified lead and is excluded from factual claims.
- Eight- versus twelve-week schedules are obsolete planning variants, not empirical disagreements.

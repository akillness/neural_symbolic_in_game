# 중요한점은 논문의 타깃은 sci, sci-e 급 저널을 타깃으로할꺼야

## Question

중요한점은 논문의 타깃은 sci, sci-e 급 저널을 타깃으로할꺼야

## Answer

The primary venue candidate is IEEE Transactions on Games, with Knowledge-Based Systems as the method-first alternative. IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing is conditional on an independently strong affect contribution; Entertainment Computing is the application/user-study alternative. Submission requires preregistered endpoints, prospective power, held-out worlds/quests, mixed-effects analysis, effect sizes and intervals, independent human and semantic audits, trace-complete artifacts, and a fresh Clarivate Master Journal List SCIE check.
